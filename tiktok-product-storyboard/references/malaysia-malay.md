# Malaysian Malay TikTok Voice

Default to natural, informal Malaysian Malay that sounds spoken during the visible action.

## Style

- Use short, breathable clauses that fit the assigned seconds.
- Prefer everyday Malaysian particles and rhythm where natural: `ni`, `je`, `lah`, `kan`, `terus`, `senang`, `nampak`, `okay`.
- Light code-switching is acceptable when common in Malaysian TikTok speech, but avoid forced slang and region caricature.
- Address the immediate visual situation. Let the performer react to what the viewer can already see.
- Keep pronunciation-friendly wording and one clear thought per segment.
- Use a direct close grounded in the product, not a fabricated urgency or promotion.

## Timing logic

- Do not name a result before the proof action finishes or the result becomes visible.
- When hands are busy or the camera reveals a detail, shorten the line rather than covering every feature.
- Do not treat a detached narration paragraph as a substitute for timed dialogue.

## Three-way variation

Give each concept its own speaking personality, hook, and close. Do not repeat the same opening, proof phrase, or call-to-action with synonyms. Possible tonal separation: curious discovery; practical daily-use problem solving; calm demonstration/reaction. These are options, not fixed personas.

Never translate unsupported claims into Malay. Preserve uncertainty and product evidence exactly as in other languages.
